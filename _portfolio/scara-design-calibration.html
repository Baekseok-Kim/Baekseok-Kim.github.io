Global Zeus / 2019–2021

Z-roll module development, assembly-error compensation and torque-speed-aware trajectory optimization.

Z-roll module

Designed and built the SCARA Z-roll module, integrating the linear and rotational mechanism with its actuator arrangement.

Kinematic calibration

Extended the kinematic model with manufacturing-error parameters, including an additional angular parameter beyond the conventional DH model.

Developed a geometry-based parameter-estimation method that used the robot’s constraints without deriving a separate Jacobian. Compared workspace measurements before and after calibration.

Trajectory optimization

Increased acceleration in the low-speed region, where more motor torque was available, and reduced acceleration at higher speed.

The original same-motion comparison reported a reduction in peak torque of more than 10%. This result describes that test motion, rather than a general cycle-time improvement.
