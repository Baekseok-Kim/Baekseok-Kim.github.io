Personal project / 2019–2020

Ball-driven reduction mechanism: 9:1 prototype and a 51:1 hollow-center design.

Operating principle

An inclined input cam drives the balls axially. A retaining structure constrains each ball relative to the housing, while the profiled output surface produces reduced rotation.

Prototype development

Built a 9:1 prototype to check the operating principle. Developed a 51:1 version with a hollow center and examined the contact geometry in CAD.
