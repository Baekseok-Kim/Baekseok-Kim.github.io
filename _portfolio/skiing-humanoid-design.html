All mechanical design projects

Hanyang University / 2017–2018

Humanoid mechanical design, foot-sensor hardware and dynamic stability simulation.

Hardware development

Designed the humanoid skiing platform in Creo and developed foot-sensor hardware, connecting mechanical design with sensing for stability analysis.

Dynamics and testing

Developed a MATLAB dynamic simulator to study zero-moment-point behavior along ski trajectories. The wider project validated skiing on real slopes.

Related publications include IEEE/RSJ IROS (2018) and Sensors 22(3), 816 (2022).
