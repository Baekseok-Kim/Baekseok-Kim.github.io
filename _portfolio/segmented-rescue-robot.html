Hanyang University / 2016–2017

Tracked-module architecture, worm gearing and drivetrain packaging for disaster communication.

Mechanical architecture

Designed the mechanical structure of a segmented robot intended to support communication in disaster environments.

Drivetrain and assembly

Developed worm gearing, shafts and caterpillar-track sprockets within the module envelope.

Designed bogie-wheel and chain-sprocket arrangements and progressed the CAD assemblies to physical hardware.
