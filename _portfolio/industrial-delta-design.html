Global Zeus / 2019–2021

Actuators and passive joints, geometric calibration, sensitivity analysis and hardware validation.

Mechanical design

Led mechanical development of the company’s first industrial parallel robot, including actuator modules, passive joints and load-bearing structures.

Designed joint modules targeting IP67 and lubrication-free joints with two rotational degrees of freedom. Used Creo and Ansys through prototype fabrication, assembly and evaluation.

Geometric calibration and design analysis

Developed forward and inverse kinematics with geometric-error parameters for the modular mechanism.

Analyzed sensitivity to geometric parameters to inform mechanical design. Developed a dynamics simulator to examine velocity and torque histories.

Hardware validation

Evaluated repeatability, payload and reliability using FARO laser-tracker measurement. The videos below show the prototype, subsystem inspections and test setups.
